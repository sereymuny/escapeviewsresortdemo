Discover Escape Views Resort

Welcome to Escape Views Resort, your peaceful island retreat in Koh Sdach, Koh Kong Province, Cambodia.

Located along Union Road, our resort offers a welcoming atmosphere for travellers seeking comfort, relaxation, and memorable island experiences.

Just approximately 10 minutes from the beach, Escape Views Resort features 11 comfortable guest rooms, making it an ideal destination for couples, families, friends, and small private gatherings.

Whether you are planning a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a relaxing island holiday, we look forward to welcoming you.
